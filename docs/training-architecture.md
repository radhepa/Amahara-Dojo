# Dojo — training architecture, Chapters 1–10

**Spoiler-free. Safe for the owner to read.** Written 7 October 2026. This is the structure of the full solo programme. It defines chapter lengths, phases, weekly rhythm, session building blocks, progression rules and chapter trials. A separate follow-up writes the actual drills (steps, checkpoints, alternatives and sources) from this outline.

It contains no story details. Story-facing notes are limited to how workouts and episodes are paired.

## Non-negotiables

- **Solo only.** No partner, instructor, sparring, contact, pads held by someone else, or "find a training buddy" steps. Everything is done alone in your own space.
- **30–40 minute plans** including preparation, rests and cool-down. **45-minute hard ceiling** including pauses (existing server rule).
- **Six practices a week. Wednesday is full rest** with no check-in.
- **Every drill has standing, supported (chair) and seated versions.** Gentle and supported practice earn exactly the same rewards.
- **No absence penalties.** Missed days resume where you left off; never double up.
- **Nothing certifies ability.** Self-checks are self-reported practice records, not ranks or fighting competence.
- **No equipment required.** Default kit: a clear, non-slip space about 2 × 2 metres, a sturdy non-wheeled chair, secure footwear or bare feet on a non-slip surface. Optional: an exercise mat; a mirror or your own phone for self-review (the app never stores video).
- **Excluded from the programme:** contact or impact work, partner drills, sparring, weapons, jumping or spinning techniques, kicks above hip height, breakfalls and floor rolls, maximal strength or endurance tests.
- **Complexity before intensity.** Add sequence length before speed, speed before power. Strikes stay controlled; elbows and knees never snap straight.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Feel | Typical length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | New skill | 30–35 min | Introduce at most one new movement family |
| Tuesday | Easy review | 30 min | Familiar drills, low effort |
| Wednesday | Rest | — | No practice, no check-in |
| Thursday | Long | 35–40 min | Main volume for the week's skill |
| Friday | Skill + conditioning | 30–35 min | Skill blocks plus one conditioning or mobility block |
| Saturday | Test-style | 35–40 min | Rounds, sequences or a self-check |
| Sunday | Easy flow | 30 min | Gentle review and mobility |

**Trial weeks** (each chapter's last week): Monday–Friday are lighter review (a small deload), Saturday is the chapter trial, Sunday is easy flow.

This matches Chapter 1's existing Monday–Sunday minutes (30, 30, 0, 30–40, 30, 35, 30).

## Session anatomy

Every session: **preparation 5 min** (30 s setup + 4½ min gradual warm-up, with shoulder, hip or ankle preparation matched to the chapter) → **technique and conditioning blocks** → **cool-down and review 5 min**. Total 30–40 minutes, leaving at least five minutes of headroom under the 45-minute ceiling.

### Block formats

All rests may be extended; a block may be cut short at any time without losing the session.

| Code | Name | Length | Structure | From |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Learn | 5 min | 1:00 setup · 3 × (0:30 work / 0:30 rest) · 1:00 review | Ch1 (existing) |
| B | Build | 5 min | 0:30 setup · 4 × (0:40 work / 0:20 rest) · 0:30 review | Ch2 W4 |
| H | Hold | 5 min | 1:00 setup · 3 × (0:20–0:30 supported stance hold / equal rest) · review | Ch4 (balance), Ch9 (stance) |
| E | Form | 5 or 10 min | setup · slow run · review · count-tempo run · review | Ch6 |
| C | Round | 10 min | 1:00 setup · 3 × (2:00 work / 1:00 rest) | Ch5 W6 |
| F | Cue | 5 min | 0:30 setup · 4 × (0:40 cued responses / 0:20 rest) · 0:30 review | Ch7 W3 |
| D | Long round | 10 min | 0:30 setup · 2 × (3:00 work / 1:00 rest) · 1:30 review | Ch8 |
| G | Count | 10 min | 1:00 setup · 6:00 continuous steady pattern on the bell's count (step out to rest whenever needed) · 3:00 breathing and review | Ch9 |
| S | Strength / mobility | 5 min | 0:30 setup · 3–4 exercises × 0:40 with 0:20 transitions · review | Ch2 (core), Ch6 (strength) |

Blocks C, D and G count as two 5-minute slots. A 40-minute session is typically 5 prep + 30 of blocks + 5 cool-down.

## Progression rules

1. **One new family per week**, introduced on Monday, practised Thursday and Saturday, reviewed Tuesday and Sunday.
2. **Complexity → speed → controlled power.** Never full power, never locked joints.
3. **Both sides.** From Chapter 2, alternate lead by round; kicks always both legs.
4. **Kicks stay at or below hip height**, chair support is always offered, no hopping to recover.
5. **Effort guide:** most work at "I can speak in short sentences." No session asks for maximal effort. Strength work stops two or three repetitions before failure.
6. **Readiness check** before each session (ready / sore or tired / pain), as now. Pain means rest or gentle mobility only, never "push through."
7. **Repeat freely.** The app may offer "repeat this week" after a trial with no penalty and no lost story progress.
8. **Legacy plans stay untouched.** Started sessions keep their original drills, doses and timings.

## Gentle, supported and seated ladder

Every drill names all three versions:

- **Strikes:** standing → chair beside you for balance → seated, practising posture and arm path.
- **Defense:** standing → supported → seated torso leans for slips, seated covers and parries.
- **Kicks:** one hand on the chair throughout → seated knee lifts and extensions.
- **Forms:** full → section by section → seated upper-body form with foot taps on the count.
- **Rounds and count blocks:** walking tempo → shortened work periods → seated rhythm on the count.

## Chapter outlines

Week focus lines show what the follow-up must turn into drills. Suggested drill IDs use `c{chapter}-{name}` and get a new prefix letter on revision (`c2b-`), following the existing `w1-` / `f2-` versioning.

### Chapter 2 — Hands (6 weeks)
Goal: controlled straight strikes in the air from stance, returning to guard every time, on both leads.
- W1 Lead straight (jab): relaxed fist, path from guard, stop just short of full extension, return.
- W2 Rear straight (cross): rear-foot pivot and hip turn, balance check after each.
- W3 The 1-2 at slow then smooth tempo.
- W4 Step-and-strike: step forward with the jab; strike then step back (uses Chapter 1 footwork). Block B introduced.
- W5 Double jab (1-1-2) and the 1-2 with a step-back exit; both leads.
- W6 Trial week.
- Conditioning (Fridays, block S): dead bug, bird dog, glute bridge, wall slides / shoulder circles.
- **Trial checks:** guard returns to the same place after every strike · elbows never snap straight · feet stay apart, no lean past the front knee · the same on both leads.

### Chapter 3 — Guard and defense (6 weeks)
Goal: solo defensive movements that always return to stance; compact curved strikes in the second half.
- W1 High cover and guard recovery; parries in the air.
- W2 Slips, small, head moving over the knee and back to centre.
- W3 Shallow roll from the knees (not the waist); step-back and pivot exits.
- W4 Lead hook (short, compact, pivot); defense-into-counter pairs (slip-cross, parry-jab).
- W5 Uppercuts, short and controlled; hook and uppercut with guard return.
- W6 Trial week.
- Conditioning: thoracic rotation, squat to chair (the roll pattern), side plank, gentle neck mobility (never forced circles).
- **Trial checks:** slips return to centre over the stance · rolls bend the knees with a long back · curved strikes stay compact with guard returning · a defense-counter pair flows at slow tempo without crossing feet.
- Safety: no fast head movement; stop if dizzy.

### Chapter 4 — Kicks and balance (6 weeks)
Goal: low, controlled kicks with a stable chamber and return, both sides, support always available.
- W1 Supported single-leg balance (block H), knee raise to chamber, knee strike in the air.
- W2 Front push kick, low: chamber, extend, re-chamber, set down.
- W3 Low round kick with standing-foot pivot, shin-to-thigh height.
- W4 Low side kick and the check (shin lift).
- W5 Hands into kicks: 1-2 into low round kick; jab into front kick; check and return.
- W6 Trial week.
- Conditioning and mobility: hip flexors and adductors, side-lying leg raises, calf raises, supported single-leg holds for ankle stability.
- **Trial checks:** chamber held for two counts (support allowed) with a soft standing knee · every kick returns to chamber, then stance, without hopping · no kick above hip height · both sides.

### Chapter 5 — Combinations and rounds (8 weeks)
Goal: link hands, defense, kicks and footwork into combinations; first timed shadow rounds.
- W1 Combination grammar: 1-2-3, 1-1-2; tempo ladder (slow, smooth, crisp).
- W2 Defense inside combinations: 1-2-slip-2, parry-1-2.
- W3 Angles: pivot out or step off after a combination.
- W4 Punch-kick links: 1-2 into low round kick; front kick into 1-2.
- W5 A fixed "set sequence" of four combinations performed with rhythm (optional self-recording).
- W6 Shadow rounds introduced (block C, 3 × 2:00 / 1:00) from a printed combination menu.
- W7 Rounds with tempo changes and defensive exits.
- W8 Trial week.
- Conditioning: low lateral shuffles, calf raises, anti-rotation core.
- **Trial:** three structured 2-minute rounds plus the set sequence. **Checks:** guard home between combinations · balance after every kick · breathing steady · feet never cross.

### Chapter 6 — Practice anywhere and Form One (6 weeks)
Goal: train in small, quiet or unfamiliar spaces; build basic bodyweight strength; learn the first complete solo form.
- W1 Small-space session design (about 1 × 1 m), quiet practice (no stamping); Form One section A.
- W2 Strength I (block S): squat to chair, incline push-up (wall or table), glute bridge, plank; Form One section B.
- W3 Strength II: chair-supported split squat, side plank, calf raise; Form One whole, slow.
- W4 Mobility flows (hips, thoracic spine, ankles, wrists) as warm-up and cool-down; Form One at count tempo.
- W5 A travel template that works with a wall or doorframe instead of a chair; Form One mirrored.
- W6 Trial week.
- **Form One:** about 60–90 seconds built only from Chapters 1–5 (stance, guard, steps, 1-2, parry, slip, hook, knee raise, front kick, cover, closing posture).
- **Trial checks:** Form One from memory at slow and count tempo · stance rebuilt between sections · balance through the kick · the strength block completed with consistent form.

### Chapter 7 — The Lantern Form and timing (8 weeks)
Goal: a longer four-section solo form and sharper timing through audio cues.
- W1 Section 1; controlled elbow strikes (horizontal and upward) inside the form.
- W2 Section 2 (turns and pivots); practising to the app's steady count.
- W3 Cue drills introduced (block F): the bell calls a cue, you answer with an assigned technique.
- W4 Section 3; cue drills with defensive answers.
- W5 Tempo ladder: the form at slow, count and smooth tempos.
- W6 Section 4; full form slow; mixed strike and kick cues.
- W7 Full form at count tempo; cues combined with the combination menu.
- W8 Trial week.
- **Trial:** the full form at two tempos from memory plus one cue-reaction set (self-rated accuracy). **Checks:** sections connect without pausing to remember · stance rebuilt at section ends · turns without crossing feet · elbows compact.
- Implementation needed: a randomised cue scheduler on the existing bell (same audio-clock approach, visual cue for muted devices).

### Chapter 8 — Endurance and composure (6 weeks)
Goal: keep technique clean over longer work periods; breathe and stay calm when tired.
- W1 Breathing skills (extended exhale, box breathing in rests); easy rhythmic shadow work; block D introduced.
- W2 Steady continuous shadow work, 4–6 minutes at easy-to-moderate effort.
- W3 Long rounds (3:00 / 1:00) from the combination menu with an effort guide.
- W4 Composure: the form and cue drills late in a session at easy intensity.
- W5 The chapter's fullest week, still within the time cap. Quality rule: if form breaks, slow down or stop.
- W6 Lighter recovery week; trial on Saturday.
- **Trial:** a steady composure session. **Checks:** rounds completed with form intact · breath settles within each rest · effort never past "short sentences" · you slowed down when form slipped.
- Safety: hydration and heat notes; stop for dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness and seek medical advice.

### Chapter 9 — Integration: steady count (8 weeks)
Goal: bring every family together in long, steady, rhythmic practice; hold stance and form with calm breathing.
- W1 Count blocks (block G): continuous base patterns (stance, guard, steps) on a steady bell count.
- W2 Supported stance holds with breathing (block H) alongside count blocks.
- W3 The Lantern Form after rounds, under mild fatigue.
- W4 Shadow rounds with intent: self-chosen combinations including defense.
- W5 Full integration: count block, rounds and form in one session.
- W6 Taper: less volume, more rest, crisp technique.
- W7 The programme's strongest week, still 30–40 minutes per session.
- W8 Lighter recovery week; trial on Saturday.
- **Trial:** a long steady-count session: one count block, the full form, two rounds, long cool-down. **Checks:** the count held without losing rhythm (rests allowed) · the form from memory when mildly tired · steady breathing · self-chosen rounds stay controlled.
- Implementation needed: a steady count mode for the bell (metronome-like, adjustable tempo, visual pulse).

### Chapter 10 — Practice for life (6 weeks)
Goal: consolidate, learn to build your own sessions, and leave with a plan you can keep.
- W1 Recovery week; light review of every family.
- W2 Self-review of each family against its checks.
- W3 Build your own session inside guardrails (preparation, 4–6 blocks, cool-down, 30–40 min); the app suggests and validates.
- W4 Maintenance templates for 3, 4 and 6 days a week.
- W5 Optional "teach it back" reflection: explain one technique in your own words. Polish a favourite form section.
- W6 Final trial: your own designed session plus the full form and a written reflection (optional, equally rewarded).
- **After Chapter 10:** a continuing-practice mode that rotates plans from every chapter, with a lighter review week every 6–8 weeks and the option to replay any chapter's curriculum.

## Self-checks, trials and milestones

- **Weekly self-check:** two checkpoints per drill, self-reported, as now.
- **Chapter trial:** the final Saturday's session, with a short checklist. Recording yourself on your own device is optional. Results are recorded honestly and **never block story episodes**.
- **Solo milestones replace instructor-based Levels 2–10.** The current `lib/levels.ts` and the Journey roadmap require instructors, coached hours and supervised partner drills, which this solo programme does not have. Replace them with ten practice milestones (Foundations, Hands, Guard, Kicks, Combinations, Anywhere, Form, Endurance, Steady Count, Lifelong), each earned by completing the chapter's practices and its trial self-check. Wording: "This records your practice and self-review. It is not a rank or a measure of fighting ability."

## How workouts and episodes connect (spoiler-free)

- One completed full or gentle practice unlocks one story episode, as now. Two short story openings each week come before the Monday and Thursday practices.
- Episodes are written to match the workout: the longest sessions (Thursday, Saturday) unlock the biggest episodes; the easy days (Tuesday, Sunday) unlock lighter, warmer ones.
- Chapter trial weeks line up with each chapter's finale.
- The characters practise the same families you do, using the same names (the count, the 1-2, the slip, the chamber, Form One, the Lantern Form). The story never comments on your real form, attendance or notes, and never awards a rank.
