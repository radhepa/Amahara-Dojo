# Chapter 5 — Borrowed Ground

**AUTHOR ONLY. Revision 3 final treatment, 7 October 2026. Eight weeks, 48 episodes, 16 openings.**

**Training sync:** Combinations and rounds (punch combinations, punch-into-kick, defense into counter, angles, timed shadow rounds). In the fiction the cohort gives its first public forms showing.

## Chapter contract

The tournament arc. Schools from three countries come to Amahara for a parts comparison and a sporting exchange, and the world becomes larger and more fun before it hardens. The rookies perform in public for the first time. Seiran loses a disputed match with dignity. Shigure's money is exposed and he answers with true receipts that tell a lie. Then the Supply Directorate's commander arrives, and in one minute of silence shows everyone what a single will can hold. The chapter ends with an invitation that is also a dare: come and see what your safety cost.

New speakers: Elian (W1), Neris in person (W2), Tarek (W3), Maela (W6). Avel and Neris speak in Veyr dialect only when they choose; never translate their private talk for the player.

## Week 1 — The Visitor's Ground

- **Opening A** — Lantern Hall prepares to host. Scrubbing, painting the guest shelf, Akari's label gun, Emi's better label gun. Montage comedy.
- **1 ●** Elian Roe arrives from Tideglass Hall with a travel-worn practice frame and a written equipment list. Their footwork is built for ground that moves.
- **2 ♪** Avel greets Elian in the dialect she chooses, then refuses to be the host of every foreign visitor. Elian apologises at once. They bicker about slang for ten minutes and are friends by the end.
- **Opening B** — Ren admires Elian's footwork and overestimates what fits into a short demonstration. Elian: "Which task is actually agreed?"
- **3 ★** Akari states contact limits and anchor boundaries; Elian asks for translation and an equal right to call stop; the printed rules change. Next dawn the apprentice finds Elian doing a different count on the dock-like edge of the courtyard wall: "Where I'm from, the ground moves. So we count with it."
- **4 ●** Elian mentions their first teacher at Tideglass: "The best I've ever seen. She left to build pumps." No name. Elian changes the subject.
- **5 ◆** Upstream schools arrive in numbers and the hall overflows. Emi's capacity management debuts. Chapter 1 `access` flag shapes the response (open afternoon with a quiet corner / appointments with flexible requests).
- **6 ☾** A meal across languages. Nobody understands every joke; everyone laughs at the right ones.
- *Sting:* three styles of shoes at the door.

## Week 2 — The Ledger From Below

- **Opening A** — Neris Vale, the registrar who wrote back to Avel, arrives with authenticated copies and a chain of custody. She keeps named sources private under explicit agreement.
- **1 ●** The ledger matches Kanna's survey and shows later shortages. It does not prove every Veyr crop failure came from Hoshin, and Neris says so first.
- **2 ♪** Sora and Neris, two record-keepers who instantly respect and annoy each other, wage a silent war of pencil against pen across one table. Toma referees. Pen wins on a technicality.
- **Opening B** — Sora wants all supporting names; Neris refuses; they negotiate independent verification instead.
- **3 ★** Kanna, here as engineer, cross-checks the dates: the transfers continued after the flood, in later high water, under Shigure's inherited contract. His contract cannot be called purely historical any more.
- **4 ●** Part of the ledger becomes public burden figures. Technical detail stays restricted. No source loses protection for a dramatic reveal.
- **5 ◆** Shigure presents relief receipts that accurately show lives saved. A crowd claps. Sora: "Where did the money come from?" The clapping stops one person at a time.
- **6 ☾** Avel and Neris talk about Merrow Quay in dialect. The apprentice understands none of it and sees Avel cry and then laugh. (C2-B `copy` colours Avel's first line to the apprentice afterwards.)
- *Sting:* the ledger's line: *Merrow Q. — 31.*

## Week 3 — The Price of Neutral Parts

- **Opening A** — Tarek Sen arrives from Cairn House carrying access stools before ceremonial gifts.
- **1 ●** Ordan's manifest: isolation gates were available and excluded from the cheap package. "Neutral" suppliers profited without making the final choice.
- **2 ♪** Food war. Tarek's plateau stew against Ren's bread; Toma judges; a tie is declared to prevent an international incident.
- **Opening B** — Riku asks who funds replacement hardware and inspections. Tarek admits his hall takes a share of guild revenue.
- **3 ★** Natsume identifies the committee recommendation she accepted. Three countries' separate compliance records are starting to fit one arrangement nobody answered for.
- **4 ●** A joint technical session reproduces the load shift on an isolated model. Tideglass, Cairn House and White Ash each solve part of it differently.
- **5 ◆** Ren feeds every working crew all day and gets the meal count exactly right. Seiran gets the public applause for the session. Ren's envy is visible; he brings the last tray anyway.
- **6 ☾** Midnight, washing pots. Ren admits to the apprentice that he wants to be clapped for. (Reflection: unflagged.)
- *Sting:* clean pots stacked in a tower.

## Week 4 — The Disputed Restart

- **Opening A** — The exhibition bracket goes up, and beside it the rookie forms showing scheduled for next week. The cohort panics in four distinct styles.
- **1 ●** Seiran vs Elian begins: White Ash's square precision against Tideglass's moving ground.
- **2 ♪** The cohort rehearses secretly in the woodshed at night. Ren catches them, pretends to be outraged, and coaches them until the lamp runs out.
- **Opening B** — An ambiguous translated restart gives Seiran an early point.
- **3 ★◆** Elian calls stop; the officials compare written versions; the point is annulled under the agreed appeal. Seiran accepts it angrily and precisely. The corrected bout ends in a narrow Elian win. Haru watches Seiran remain a good instructor without a flawless result to stand behind.
- **4 ●** Emi halts an overcapacity spectator queue. Two famous instructors are turned away and thank her.
- **5 ◆** Seiran and Elian help dismantle the frame and argue cheerfully over dinner; an arm-wrestle ends in a tie neither accepts. A rivalry-friendship that will outlast a war.
- **6 ☾** Seiran, alone afterwards, asks the apprentice — of everyone — "Was the annulment fair?" (Reflection: unflagged; she listens to the whole answer.)
- *Sting:* the scoreboard with one point crossed out.

## Week 5 — The First Showing

- **Opening A** — Morning of the rookie showing. Emi's hands shake; Jalen over-recites; Avel is calm as a needle; the apprentice feels everything.
- **1 ● Decision C5-A `forms`.** The cohort draws order and the apprentice may volunteer to go first and steady the others (`first`) or take the last slot and close (`last`).
- **2 ♪** Warm-ups. Ren's pep talk is a disaster; Daichi's is a pun; Akari's is one sentence: "You already practised alone. Do it where we can see."
- **Opening B** — The crowd: three countries, Seiran and Elian in the front, Kanna with a notebook, Toma on a barrel.
- **3 ★◆ The showing.** The cohort performs the base form with combinations, one by one. Describe each in a few lines; describe the apprentice's with care and no claim of mastery, only timing that holds. Under their last step the floor stone rings, faint and clean. (`first`: the others steady because they saw it. `last`: the apprentice closes what three friends opened.) The cohort hugs in a heap.
- **4 ●** Founders' reactions: Ren cries openly; Akari straightens a cup that isn't crooked; Sora writes the time down; Daichi says nothing and is the first to the door to meet them; Yuzu folds four paper moths.
- **5 ◆** Neris, now speaking as a seconded treaty officer, separates Veyr's legitimate claim from the Directorate's emergency powers. Shigure proposes a centralised repair purchase that gives him exclusive custody of route records. Mika asks whether a clinic can refuse data enrolment and still receive aid. The form doesn't protect that refusal.
- **6 ☾** Daichi declines Shigure's request for a private endorsement. Their shared civic history makes it painful, not heroic. (Personal-scene hook: a night walk after the showing, romance or friendship variant.)
- *Sting:* the apprentice's footprint in chalk dust on the floor.

## Week 6 — The Commander

- **Opening A** — A Directorate supply convoy escorts the Veyr delegation's equipment into town. A woman steps down from the first wagon. Elian goes very still. (Natsume left for a week of restitution work at Weir End the day before; she returns only for the closing in Week 8.)
- **1 ●** Commander Maela Vorr: polite operational clarity, wasted movement nowhere. She thanks the hosts, asks to see the Weir End survey in full, and already knows the public numbers by heart.
- **2 ♪** Easy day. The cohort sees Maela at the bakery stall buying bread. She is ordinary and kind to Toma, who drops his coins. Avel: "She pulled my neighbour out of the water." Complexity, not comedy, is this filler's sincere line.
- **Opening B** — The exhibition committee, flattered, asks the commander to demonstrate.
- **3 ★◆ Rookie awe, inverted.** Alone, Maela holds an isolated resonance across the whole practice yard — work that takes a trained pair — for one full minute. The ring is enormous and perfectly clean. Frames hum. Nobody breathes. Seiran pales; Akari grips her clipboard until the edge marks her palm; Elian looks away. Maela steps off without sweat. "Most people never learn to be enough by themselves."
- **4 ●** Elian and Maela speak in dialect. Elian refuses an invitation to rejoin her. Avel, who understands, translates nothing.
- **5 ◆** Maela finds the apprentice. She watched the rookie showing. "You count like someone who practises alone. Good." The apprentice feels praised and frightened in the same breath. (Thesis seed: alone versus together.)
- **6 ☾** Night. Akari can't sleep; neither can Seiran; they meet at the empty practice yard and, for once, agree. "If she ever holds something real like that—" "—nobody could stop her."
- *Sting:* the yard where she stood: no footprints. She didn't move.

## Week 7 — The Money Someone Else Saved

- **Opening A** — The audit (Sora and Neris) and the parts comparison (Riku and Tarek) meet: deferred isolation spending financed visible relief. Shigure knew after inheriting the contract and chose concealment, repeatedly.
- **1 ●** Shigure publishes selected true receipts before the full account and casts the reformers as people who would cancel medicine for a principle.
- **2 ♪** River-tune night with every visiting school. Maela doesn't attend. Neris does and sings magnificently off-key. Yuzu leads; Tarek's students drum on stools. Joy in the middle of a fight.
- **Opening B** — Sora's earlier statement circulates, accurately quoted and stripped of its uncertainty. C2-A `stamp`: `keep` — the apprentice hands her stamp back so she can stamp her correction; `return` — a heckler points at the stamp on her cord and she doesn't hide it.
- **3 ★** Sora issues a specific correction and cannot force anyone to accept it. A household receiving aid disputes the inquiry's priorities; Mika agrees a patient can't wait for a perfect institution and still opposes compulsory record access.
- **4 ● Decision C5-B `offer`.** Maela, leaving the archive, offers the apprentice a place at the Directorate's responder school. "In three years you would be extraordinary." Decline with thanks (`thanks`) or ask a question first: "Who decides where your students are sent?" (`question`) — "I do."
- **5 ◆** The coalition protects current deliveries with open temporary contributions. Riku refuses an unpaid replacement schedule that would move the debt onto repair workers. Shigure's policy stays lawful pending review; his credibility splits the town.
- **6 ☾** Shigure eating noodles alone at a stall. Daichi sits two stools away. They don't speak. Daichi pays for both bowls.
- *Sting:* two empty bowls on the counter.

## Week 8 — A Smaller Departure (chapter trial)

- **Opening A** — Trial morning: combinations and rounds for the cohort and visiting juniors. Results recorded honestly; Jalen is not perfect and says so first.
- **1 ●** The gathering closes. Supplies counted; matches recorded separately from civic agreements. Respect between schools has not settled any river.
- **2 ♪** Farewells. Seiran and Elian's rematch arm-wrestle (tie again). Tarek invites everyone to Cairn House's hot springs: "Bring towels. Sora, bring three."
- **Opening B** — Neris learns her registry post is under review for bringing the ledger. She keeps travelling.
- **3 ★** Maela, departing, invites the delegation to Veyr. "Come and look at what your safety cost." Not a threat; an invitation that is also a dare. Natsume answers before Akari can: "I'll come." It is the first time the two women have been in the same room. Maela looks at her directly, once. "Good. Come and read them." Nobody else understands what she means yet.
- **4 ●** Who goes: Akari, Sora, Daichi, Yuzu, Riku, Natsume, Seiran (as academy observer), Jalen and the apprentice; Ren for the first leg only, then home to the bakery. Emi stays to run the paid welcome shift. Avel chooses to stay: "I left Merrow once. I don't need to see it to know it."
- **5 ◆** Yuzu hands Emi the hall key and a return plan. The people staying begin supper before the road party is out of sight.
- **6 ☾** On the road at dusk the apprentice looks back at Lantern Hall's lantern. The bell rings two. Beside them, Natsume flinches.
- *Sting:* the road east.

## Handoff
