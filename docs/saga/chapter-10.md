# Chapter 10 — What We Leave Open

**AUTHOR ONLY. Revision 3 final treatment, 7 October 2026. Six weeks, 36 episodes, 12 openings. The saga's final chapter.**

**Training sync:** Practice for life (consolidation, building your own session, a sustainable plan, a final self-review of everything learned). In the fiction the hall reopens and its first full dawn count includes Ren, standing.

## Chapter contract

The ending treats peace with the same care earlier chapters gave conflict, without turning into a committee. Trials do not repair houses; reparations do not bring Natsume back; love does not cure a hand. The hall is rebuilt with something from every door. Iori visits on a day he chose, with his own bowl. Ren stands for the dawn count. Akari takes a walk. Yuzu packs. And the saga ends where it began: a newcomer at a door that sticks, a bench in the way, and the apprentice there to welcome them.

No new enemy, no secret mastermind, no recovered Natsume, no sequel hook.

## Week 1 — A Budget Is Also a Promise

- **Opening A** — The reconstruction list, read aloud in the shelter: lost buildings, clinical adaptations, ordinary housing, Lantern Hall's bell or floor. Nobody calls any of it a monument.
- **1 ●** Hoshin's former contract money enters a supervised restitution account; downstream representatives decide how their repairs are done. Kanna reads the first payment notice and puts it in a drawer without comment, which from Kanna is joy.
- **2 ♪** The cohort's first paid jobs. Emi's welcome shift, Avel's translation table, Jalen's responder-candidate hours. They compare wages over noodles and argue about who pays.
- **Opening B** — Ordan's guild transfers parts without the old hidden surcharge; Tarek's hall gets open class support. Veyr's civic delegates fund their own repairs and contest emergency powers at home; nobody in Amahara claims credit for Veyr's reforms.
- **3 ★ The rebuild.** C9-A `last-step`.
  - `forward` (the bell cracked): the bell will be recast, and every door that held the Line is invited to send one piece of metal. Hinges, keys, a buckle, Toma's festival prize, a tray from Ren's bakery. Akari brings the brass cap from Natsume's inspection pencil and puts it in the crucible herself.
  - `back` (the floor split): a new floor, one board from every door that held the Line, each with its lantern word burned into the underside. Akari brings a board from the Old Gate's fence.
- **4 ●** Riku runs the job with paid apprentices and refuses an impossible quote from a council clerk, politely and completely.
- **5 ◆** Chapter 1 `repair` flag: the first piece placed is in the order the hall chose on the apprentice's first week (floor first / welcome corner first). The apprentice is asked to place it.
- **6 ☾** Mika and Daichi on the shelter steps. She is angry at a clinic tool she can no longer use and says so for ten full minutes. He listens and fixes nothing.
- *Sting:* the crucible glowing, or a stack of boards with words on their undersides.

## Week 2 — The Testimony That Is Not a Duel (trials)

- **Opening A** — Shigure's hearing distinguishes inherited design, deliberate later concealment and the lease. His relief receipts mitigate some claims and cancel none.
- **1 ●** Affected residents testify in person or by written submission; private submission is treated as equal grief, not weaker evidence.
- **2 ☾** Natsume's signed confession and the Old Gate records are presented together. Her death neither erases her culpability nor lets officials blame every decision on her absence. Akari sits through it without moving her hands.
- **Opening B** — Maela's trial: unlawful occupation, cancelled suspension rights, the anchor order during the evacuation. Her competent supply work is evidence, not immunity.
- **3 ★◆ Iori.** By his own choice, Iori testifies at Maela's trial. Not about her crimes; about the night. "The river took my leg at Weir End the same night it took your brother at Merrow. You decided the answer was to choose who it takes next. I decided nobody gets to choose that alone." Maela looks at him for a long time. She does not apologise and does not look away. It is the only time in the saga she is matched.
- **4 ●** C5-B `offer`: Maela's single line to the apprentice in the corridor. `thanks`: "You turned me down politely. I thought you'd change your mind when you saw what the world costs. You held a door instead." `question`: "You asked who decides. I said I do. I'm still not certain I was wrong. But I didn't decide this."
- **5 ◆** Judgments. Shigure loses all contract authority; restitution and restricted service, assigned to Weir End under Kanna's supervision (she hates it; he complies). Maela is sentenced and remains detained after appeal. No protagonist delivers private punishment.
- **6 ☾** Coda: Maela at dawn in a detention yard, practising her solo form alone. Far off, a door chime rings three. She pauses for exactly one count, then continues.
- *Sting:* her footprints in frost. This time, she moved.

## Week 3 — People Learn Somewhere Else First

- **Opening A** — White Ash resumes in a borrowed hall. Seiran runs a written refusal appeal with the students who once waited for permission. A first-year uses it on her within the hour; she grants it.
- **1 ●** Jalen begins supervised instructor preparation and lists the things he is not yet authorised to teach. C3-A `give`: at his induction he thanks the apprentice for the turn they gave him at White Ash, counting "one, two" under his breath.
- **2 ♪** Mika finds an adapted tool she loves and is insufferable about it. She delegates a procedure she can no longer do and goes to the window afterwards. Frustration and competence in the same scene.
- **Opening B** — Ren's physiotherapist clears him for adapted standing practice. He tells no one and turns up at the shelter yard at dawn.
- **3 ★ Decision C10-A `standing`.** Ren's first standing count since the storm. He asks the apprentice to count for him (`count`), or the apprentice stands beside him and does it too, silently (`beside`). No comeback, no full power, no pain denial: the base count, slowly, on his own feet. C4-B `corner`: Haru, watching from the gate, says the word and then leaves before Ren can be embarrassed.
- **4 ●** Toma's letter to the apprentice (C8-A: "You gave me a real job." / "You told me you were scared too.") and his choice of study: bell-casting (`forward`) or carpentry (`back`). Messaging is no longer his whole identity. He leaves the message bag on its hook.
- **5 ◆** The apprentice receives their next beginner-level practice plan, the same as anyone's. The story says nothing about rank; the fiction's only comment is Daichi asking if they want tea.
- **6 ☾** The cohort at the river at night, throwing stones. Emi asks what they'll do when the story stops feeling like a story. Nobody knows. It's fine.
- *Sting:* four stones skipping, one sinking at once (Jalen's).

## Week 4 — Common Ground

- **Opening A** — The Common Routes Compact is drafted in one long, ordinary week: public burden limits, funded maintenance, protected technical access, local suspension, bounded emergency terms, independent inspection, civilian appeals. Kanna strikes a clause that would make downstream refusal too expensive to use; Akari backs her though it slows an upstream reopening.
- **1 ●** Seiran demands qualification standards; Emi demands plain language. Both go in. Neris secures Veyr civic ratification through its own delegates; Ordan's cantons ratify on different dates. It enters force with review dates and paid appeal staff.
- **2 ♪ River day.** The whole cast at the river. Seiran and Elian's rematch becomes a swimming race and then a tie and then an argument. Daichi and Tarek's pun war. Akari, in patterned cloth, finally says out loud that she likes it. Neris sings. Sora lends a stranger her pencil and lets them annotate her map while she holds it steady. Avel teaches the cohort a Merrow song and doesn't stop when people listen.
- **Opening B** — The Compact's first seasonal test: a household stop inconveniences freight.
- **3 ★** A provisional coordinator tries to overrule the stop out of old emergency habit. Seiran refuses the invalid command under the new charter. Riku's crews fix the access fault with funded parts; Tarek and Elian keep limited isolated timing.
- **4 ●** A corrected public account names the delay and who bore its cost. Sora adds a factual amendment about her own section before anyone asks.
- **5 ◆** The household agrees to reopen after inspection; the three-note clearance confirms a route that stayed legitimately closed until then. Freight is late; somebody complains loudly at the market; nobody is sacrificed.
- **6 ☾** Akari and Seiran share a drink and don't check anything.
- *Sting:* the complaint letter pinned to the board, answered.

## Week 5 — A Visit That Is Not Forgiveness

- **Opening A** — Iori arrives on a day he chose, with his own bowl and a workshop errand (Chapter 1 `place`: he sets it beside the blue bowl kept for him, or among the bowls for whoever arrives, and says which he'd have picked).
- **1 ●** Daichi asks whether Iori wants to see the repaired beam. Iori agrees, looks for a long time, then changes the subject to what the brace cost.
- **2 ♪** They repair a kettle together and disagree about a measurement until Riku arrives and says they're both wrong. Daichi asks before improvising. The kettle still needs Riku.
- **Opening B** — Iori watches the dawn count in the rebuilt hall. C4-A `chamber`: he watches the apprentice kick and says "Better." (`learn`), or watches Daichi and says it to him (`decline`). It is the only praise Iori gives in the saga.
- **3 ★** Iori says he can meet again. He does not offer complete forgiveness. Daichi accepts the real relationship instead of waiting for a cleansing sentence. Kanna, there for a separate meeting, does not manage either of them.
- **4 ●** Akari visits Natsume's memorial record (C8-B `memorial` form) and adds an amendment from an affected Weir End family, in her own handwriting. C3-B `seat`: she reads it standing where she stood at White Ash.
- **5 ◆** Akari leaves the morning opening to Emi and takes a walk. A crooked cup stays crooked all day. Nothing goes wrong. She comes back and does not check.
- **6 ☾** Mika joins supper with the utensil she prefers. Nobody remarks on it.
- *Sting:* the crooked cup.

## Week 6 — An Ordinary Open Door (finale)

- **Opening A** — Lantern Hall reopens. Its first full dawn count with everyone: founders, cohort, Riku, Mika, Haru, Seiran and Elian visiting, Kanna and Iori, Toma at the end of the row trying not to look proud. Ren standing.
- **1 ●** `forward`: the recast bell is hung, not yet rung; its frame footing is still curing. `back`: the new floor's first practice; someone finds their door's word under a board and won't say which; one last strip by the far wall is unfinished. Either way Riku strings a yellow cord around the unfinished part, and the apprentice notices.
- **2 ♪** Yuzu packs for a touring contract with real home dates. She leaves an actual spare key and asks someone to water a plant instead of admiring a paper moth. Ren pretends to cry; then actually does.
- **Opening B** — The final shared afternoon is set up. Every Chapter 1 promise has an object you can point to: the repair board, the access sign, the corrected notice, the named jobs, the bowl, the testimony, the inquiry's copies.
- **3 ★** Founder relationships resolve, romance or friendship by the player's state: a date, a walk, tea that fits real schedules. Akari–Ren and Daichi–Mika resolve by their own rules if available. C7-B `festival`: the apprentice's seat at the afternoon is beside that person (or the cohort).
- **4 ●** The afternoon: Elian and Seiran argue about the disputed restart over Ren's bread; Kanna and Iori discuss different projects without becoming a reconciliation unit; Avel's cooperative signs a contract; Jalen describes a skill he must still demonstrate; Emi opens the ledger; Sora's map has three handwritings on it. C2-A `stamp`: the courier stamp hangs on the wall by the door (`keep`) or Sora retires it to the record circle's box (`return`).
- **5 ◆** Dusk. Everyone has gone. The apprentice is alone in the hall and lights the lantern, the way Daichi did on the first dawn of Chapter 2. Light your own lantern first. A short, wordless solo count in an empty room that is full of everyone.
- **6 ★ The open door. Decision C10-B `place`.** Next morning a newcomer comes up the hill. The gate sticks; someone has left a bench in the way. The apprentice is at the door (`door`) or at the table pulling out a chair (`table`).
  - `door`: "Come around the bench. The yellow cord is a boundary, not a decoration." (Riku has strung a yellow cord around the last unfinished work: the bell frame's footing (`forward`) or the final strip of new boards (`back`). Day one, exactly, from the other side of the door.)
  - `table`: a chair is already pulled out; "Sit wherever you like. Tell me when an instruction isn't clear."
  - The newcomer reads the word on the hall's lantern (C7-A). Three notes ring from an inspected route: from the recast bell, for the first time (`forward`), or from the old bell over the new floor (`back`).
  - The newcomer asks: "Are you a teacher here?"
  - "No. I just practice here."
  - End.

## Final state

The original crime, the continuing concealment, the international burden, the occupation and the central bypass all have answers and consequences. Death, disability, displacement and incomplete forgiveness persist beside pleasure and working institutions. The five founders remain distinct people, and Lantern Hall no longer depends on their constant heroism. The apprentice belongs without being exceptional, romantic, publicly expressive, always present or physically advanced. End here.
