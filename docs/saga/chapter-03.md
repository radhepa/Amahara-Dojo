# Chapter 3 — White Ash

**AUTHOR ONLY. Complete treatment; not yet playable.**

## Chapter contract

White Ash Academy is worth respecting. Its teachers protect responders with clear tasks, well-kept equipment, and practiced partners. Its hierarchy also discourages the person at the edge of a plan from admitting that the plan is wrong. Seiran tests Lantern Hall's informal habits; Akari tests Seiran's authority. Natsume's confession gives the dispute a human consequence and turns a suspected omission into an admitted official act. Haru and Ren's rivalry becomes a disagreement about what competence owes other people.

## Eight episode units

### Episode 1 — A school that works

1. Seiran greets the visitors through a practical orientation. Clear lanes and supported preparation make the academy's competence visible before any ideological clash.
2. Jalen sees bursary places that an independent hall cannot provide. Ren recognizes the same techniques Haru practices and resents being treated as an informal guest.
3. Seiran checks the visitors' actual experience and assigns observation roles. The apprentice's attendance does not qualify them for partner contact or live anchors.
4. Akari objects to a blanket permission form while acknowledging the useful limits inside it. Seiran offers a specific amended task rather than demanding surrender.
5. Emi asks for an accessible written explanation of the drill. A junior academy group admits that they also wanted it.
6. A model exercise succeeds beautifully. The result makes it costly, not easy, to question the structure that produced it.

### Episode 2 — The command between movements

1. Haru and Ren join a licensed isolated-anchor drill under stated limits. Their paired timing is excellent when the task is actually defined.
2. A scheduled controller change issues an order whose stop condition does not match the lane. The fault comes from a revised instruction, not planted sabotage.
3. Haru repeats the command, believing its upstream clearance valid. Ren notices the returning load could reach a waiting group and calls the stop.
4. Seiran halts the drill before harm. She disputes Ren's authorization until the revised sheet proves nobody had been assigned the relevant decision.
5. The debrief format is chosen: reconstruct the command chain or hear participant accounts first [C03-A]. Both include documents and people, in different order.
6. Haru realizes he defended an instruction partly because Ren challenged it. He cannot turn the apology into a lesson for his younger brother.

### Episode 3 — What an order leaves out

1. The selected debrief exposes either an unassigned role or a unheard warning first. Seiran agrees the gap is real while resisting Lantern Hall's preferred remedy.
2. Jalen asks whether refusing the same order would jeopardize his application. An instructor's reassuring intention cannot answer a missing written protection.
3. Avel explains that a translated command omitted a conditional word. She will check the phrase, not certify every international order the school has ever used.
4. Akari has quietly checked Emi's work again. Emi asks why another hall's obedience troubles her while her own delegation remains reversible.
5. Haru stays after practice to dismantle the test frame with Ren. Neither asks a winner to be declared.
6. Seiran writes a provisional stop appeal into the visiting drill. Its limits will be tested again when the academy faces a real emergency.

### Episode 4 — The signature

1. Natsume appears at the promised evidence forum: town workshop or academy session according to Chapter 2. The venue changes who feels entitled to ask first.
2. She confirms that she signed the narrow market-success report and withheld the downstream diagram. Her claim that secrecy prevented raids is examined, not accepted wholesale.
3. Sora's copies establish that the omission persisted after the emergency. Natsume admits she also protected her own judgment and the council's account.
4. Akari asks one precise question about people downstream. Natsume answers it before speaking about family, refusing to make niece and inspector interchangeable.
5. Follow-up testimony is structured by affected topics or chronological reconstruction [C03-B]. Either gives participants a way to submit privately without disappearing from the record.
6. The forum records culpability but cannot yet prove every physical consequence. Natsume agrees to independent review and temporarily steps back from signing final route approvals.

### Episode 5 — A family cannot hear for a town

1. Akari declines a family-only explanation before the public record is finished. Later she accepts tea, unable to decide whether the ordinary gesture feels disloyal.
2. Ren tries a joke that implies confession fixes everything. Mika asks him who still needs a working gate; he retracts it without theatrical self-condemnation.
3. Shigure says qualified oversight is now more necessary than ever. His criticism of improvised halls is valid even as his own accounts remain closed.
4. Seiran offers a monitored training exchange that Lantern Hall can afford. Akari requests a stop right and written scope instead of rejecting institutional help outright.
5. Haru admits to Ren that prestige made the bad instruction easier to trust. Ren admits he sometimes challenges authority because someone else receives the applause.
6. A quiet evening lets the brothers cook together. The food is good; emotional growth need not require another burned tray.

### Episode 6 — An answer with a gate

1. Riku demonstrates a model isolation gate, explaining why the old plan would have needed more of them. Natsume knows a manual fallback procedure.
2. Jalen asks why cheaper central parts were selected. Natsume can name the purchasing committee but has no complete spending ledger.
3. The stopped drill is repeated with an explicit local refusal. Seiran follows that stop and the system succeeds without crowning Lantern Hall's method universally superior.
4. Emi asks what the academy does when an appeal is inconvenient rather than urgent. Seiran has to promise a process her superiors have not yet approved.
5. The exchange priority is chosen: observer access or written refusal protection [C03-C]. Both commitments must eventually exist; the first receives limited available work.
6. A dated engineering request arrives signed by Kanna. It asks for measurements at the river, not agreement with Lantern Hall's interpretation.

### Episode 7 — A refusal that costs

1. Academy administrators delay Seiran's appeal proposal, citing responsibility to the central fund. Her first consequence is administrative, not an instant defection to the founders.
2. Shigure makes continued parts access contingent on a clear accountable operator. Riku demands that the responsibility include funding for maintenance.
3. Natsume offers survey expertise under an independent engineer's supervision. Akari cannot decide for downstream residents whether that contribution is acceptable.
4. Daichi receives permission from Iori for a letter and a possible later meeting, explicitly excluding a public reunion. He reads only the portion he may share.
5. Haru volunteers for the river survey through the academy's actual assignment rules. Seiran approves a limited observation role and keeps a copy of the conditions.
6. The hall prepares travel work without assuming every founder must go. Yuzu's river contacts supply lodging; Ren stays for a promised bakery shift first.

### Episode 8 — The person below the diagram

1. The academy records its stopped drill as a design correction rather than a shameful exception. Its students can cite the change later.
2. Jalen keeps his application and asks for the amended refusal procedure. He is not forced to choose moral purity over an affordable career.
3. Natsume signs a statement naming her omission and the questions still open. It remains available to critics even when they dislike her present cooperation.
4. Akari and Seiran conduct a restrained rules exchange. Neither overwhelms the other; a mutual stop ends the contest with a disputed but instructive result.
5. The original page is prepared for a witnessed survey alongside independent copies. The apprentice's role is a protected record station with the right to decline travel.
6. Kanna's final condition arrives: measure the houses, not just the market. Iori adds that he will bring his own bowl if he chooses to come.

## Optional founder conversations

- Akari: mending a formal sleeve; wanting a private family account; asking what contact Natsume can accept; attending tea without defending the report.
- Ren: studying an academy score sheet; jealousy of Haru's position; requesting feedback without comparison; practicing the corrected step within a modest limit.
- Sora: conflicting pagination; an unfair criticism beside a fair one; asking permission to quote a witness; leaving an unanswered question visible.
- Daichi: packing a spare kettle; imagining Iori's welcome; asking what a visit may include; leaving an invitation unanswered for a night.
- Yuzu: returning with river tunes; being called unserious; defining a touring commitment; keeping it without apologizing for travel.

## Six supporting visits

1. Seiran checks a training scope and discusses its cost.
2. Haru apologizes without coaching Ren through the response.
3. Jalen keeps his bursary application and asks a hard procedural question.
4. Emi rehearses a plain-language explanation with academy students.
5. Natsume shares a family meal after the official record, without a forgiveness prompt.
6. Riku invoices the model gate materials instead of donating them silently.

## Handoff

Natsume's omission is admitted and reviewable. Seiran's authority has a fissure, not a collapse. Physical downstream causation still needs measurement. Kanna sets the next chapter's terms, and Iori controls the terms of his own appearance.
