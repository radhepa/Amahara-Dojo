# Chapter 3 — White Ash

**AUTHOR ONLY. Revision 3 final treatment, 7 October 2026. Six weeks, 36 episodes, 12 openings.**

**Training sync:** Guard and defense (parries, covers, the slip, the roll, step-back and pivot), then the curved hands (lead hook, uppercuts) in the second half. In the fiction Seiran gives one famous correction: the slip.

## Chapter contract

White Ash Academy deserves respect: two hundred students doing the dawn count in unison sound like rain on stone. Its hierarchy also teaches people at the edge of a plan not to say the plan is wrong. Ren calls a stop that saves the waiting group, the apprentice included. Akari meets Seiran, her equal and opposite, and a rivalry is born. Natsume confesses, and the apprentice decides where Akari sits while she does. The chapter ends with Kanna's demand to measure the houses and Daichi washing the red scarf.

New speaker: Seiran (W1).

## Week 1 — A School That Works

- **Opening A** — Half a day by cart. The cohort sleeps on sacks; Jalen recites the academy rules from memory until Avel threatens to sew his mouth shut. They arrive at dawn: the courtyard full of students doing the count in perfect unison. A sound like rain on stone.
- **1 ●** Seiran's orientation: clear lanes, labelled equipment, shoes checked at the gate (Haru warned them). Her competence is visible before any argument.
- **2 ♪** Dorm night. Emi labels the bunks; Avel mends Jalen's uniform cuff without asking; the cohort sneaks to the academy kitchen and is caught by Haru, who feeds them and swears them to secrecy. Sincere line: Haru, to the apprentice, "Ren talks about you. Don't tell him I said so."
- **Opening B** — Seiran checks each visitor's actual experience and assigns observation roles. She tells the apprentice kindly and exactly why attendance qualifies them for nothing yet. Akari objects to a blanket permission form; Seiran amends it on the spot.
- **3 ★◆ Rookie awe.** Seiran and a partner hold an isolated anchor; a ring like a struck bowl fills the hall and stays. Then Seiran demonstrates defense alone: slip, roll, return, so clean the apprentice forgets to breathe. Silence after.
- **4 ●** Emi asks for a plain written explanation of the drill. A junior group admits they wanted one too. Seiran is annoyed, then writes it, then corrects her own sentence.
- **5 ◆** Ren sees Haru's techniques taught as a curriculum with Haru as its model. Ren is "the informal guest." A sharp exchange in a corridor that neither brother wins.
- **6 ☾** Akari and Seiran at the rail at night, two people who check everything. "You check shoes." "You check people." They recognise each other and dislike it.
- *Sting:* Akari's checklist and Seiran's command sheet on the same table, squared to the same edge.

## Week 2 — The Command Between Movements

- **Opening A** — Haru and Ren join a licensed isolated-anchor drill. When the task is defined, their paired timing is beautiful; for a minute they are brothers again.
- **1 ●** A scheduled controller change issues a revised order whose stop condition does not match the lane. Haru repeats it.
- **2 ♪** Easy day. The cohort practises guard with academy juniors. The apprentice's guard drops when tired; a junior shows the trick ("elbows home, like you're carrying bread"). Jalen insists on writing it down.
- **Opening B** — The drill resumes. The cohort watches from the waiting area.
- **3 ★◆** Ren sees the returning load could reach the waiting group, the cohort and the apprentice among them, and calls STOP. Haru argues. Seiran halts the drill before harm, then disputes Ren's authority until the revised sheet proves nobody had been assigned the decision. In the waiting area the apprentice did not run and did not freeze: they stepped back on the count.
- **4 ●** Debrief. Seiran agrees the gap is real and resists Lantern Hall's preferred remedy. Jalen asks whether refusing that order would have cost him his application. Nobody can show him a written protection.
- **5 ◆ Decision C3-A `correction`.** Seiran's habit: one private correction to one visitor per exchange. She offers it to the apprentice, who "stepped back on the count." Jalen needs it for his licence component. Take it (`take`) or give it to Jalen (`give`).
  - `take`: Seiran teaches the slip-and-return. "Your head leaves. Your feet stay. Come back to where you were."
  - `give`: Jalen gets the correction; afterwards he hugs the apprentice awkwardly and counts "one, two" while he does it.
- **6 ☾** Haru realises he defended the order partly because Ren challenged it. He cannot make the apology into a lesson for his younger brother, so he says nothing. The brothers sit on the roof, not looking at each other, until Ren hands him half an apple.
- *Sting:* Seiran writing "STOP RIGHT" at the top of a blank sheet.

## Week 3 — What an Order Leaves Out

- **Opening A** — Avel finds the translated command dropped a conditional word. She will check that phrase; she will not certify every order the school has ever used.
- **1 ●** Seiran writes a provisional stop appeal into the visiting drill. It is two lines long and she rewrites it four times.
- **2 ♪** Academy sports day, open to visitors. Relays; Yuzu's invented "guard tag"; Daichi and Toma (visiting with a delivery) in the three-legged race; Akari wins a sprint by refusing to be overtaken; so does Seiran; it is a tie; both are furious. Sincere line: Seiran, grudgingly, "Your hall is fun. I don't know what to do with that."
- **Opening B** — Akari has rechecked Emi's work again. Emi calls it out in front of Seiran: "Why does their obedience trouble you when your delegation is reversible?"
- **3 ★** Akari gives Emi a real task and does not check it. Agony played for heart and comedy: Ren physically holds Akari's sleeve; Akari makes it through the whole afternoon. Emi's work is fine. Akari says so. That is the whole scene and it is enormous.
- **4 ●** Haru stays after practice to dismantle the frame with Ren. Neither asks for a winner.
- **5 ◆** Natsume arrives at White Ash for the forum. In the corridor Akari finds her aunt checking her own shoes. "Is it true?" "Tomorrow. I'll say it once, in the right room."
- **6 ☾** The night before. The apprentice finds Akari awake, mending a formal sleeve in patterned fabric she claims not to like. They talk about nothing. (Reflection: unflagged.)
- *Sting:* Natsume's statement in an envelope on a table, unopened.

## Week 4 — The Signature

- **Opening A** — Morning. Akari, holding the mended sleeve, asks the apprentice something she has never asked anyone: where should she sit?
- **1 ● Decision C3-B `seat`.** With her aunt (`family`) or among the town (`town`). Both are brave; neither is wrong. Then the walk into the forum hall. Chapter 1 `statement` flag shapes who asks first (founders' account with visitors' exact words / residents' mixed spoken and written account).
- **2 ●** The forum opens. Procedure, written questions, residents from Amahara and a Weir End delegation, Shigure in the second row. The tension is the room's breathing.
- **Opening B** — Natsume stands.
- **3 ★◆ The confession.** She signed the narrow report and withheld the downstream diagram. The raids excuse is examined, not accepted. She admits she also protected her own judgment and the council. Akari asks one precise question about the people downstream; Natsume answers it before she says anything about family. `family`: under the table Natsume's hand finds Akari's for one second and lets go. `town`: Natsume looks at her niece among her accusers and answers anyway, voice steady, hands not.
- **4 ●** The forum records culpability; it cannot yet prove every physical consequence. Natsume steps back from signing route approvals. Shigure: qualified oversight is now more necessary than ever. He is not wrong, and his own accounts stay closed.
- **5 ◆** Akari declines a family-only explanation. Later she accepts tea and cannot decide whether that is disloyal. Natsume, over the tea: "I used to think when it rang three again, I'd be forgiven. Now I think it'll only mean the gate works." Akari doesn't answer. **(Key seed for 8.5.)**
- **6 ☾** Ren tries a joke implying confession fixes everything; Mika (visiting for the forum) asks who still needs a working gate; he retracts it without theatre. The brothers cook dinner together. The food is good.
- *Sting:* Natsume alone on the academy wall, facing toward Amahara.

## Week 5 — An Answer With a Gate

- **Opening A** — Riku's model isolation gate: why the old plan needed more of them. Natsume knows the manual fallback procedure by heart and says so too quickly.
- **1 ●** Jalen asks why cheaper central parts were chosen. Natsume can name the purchasing committee but has no spending ledger.
- **2 ♪** Ren asks Haru for feedback "without comparison." Haru tries and fails three times in increasingly funny ways, then succeeds once, and Ren doesn't know what to do with his face.
- **Opening B** — The stopped drill is repeated with an explicit local refusal written in.
- **3 ★◆** Mid-drill, the refusal is called by the junior who wanted the plain explanation. Seiran obeys it instantly. The system works. Seiran's expression as she follows a first-year's stop is the most honest thing about her so far.
- **4 ●** Emi asks what the academy does when an appeal is inconvenient rather than urgent. Seiran promises a process her superiors have not approved.
- **5 ◆** Administrators delay Seiran's appeal proposal, citing the central fund. Shigure ties parts access to a single accountable operator; Riku demands maintenance funding in the same breath. That night Seiran does the dawn count at midnight, alone on the empty floor. The apprentice, sleepless, sees her. She doesn't stop. Neither speaks. Even Seiran lights her own lantern.
- **6 ☾** Daichi receives Iori's permission for a letter and a possible meeting, explicitly not a public reunion. He reads aloud only the part he may share. Mika sits with him and doesn't ask for the rest.
- *Sting:* back in Amahara, the red scarf on its beam.

## Week 6 — The Person Below the Diagram (chapter trial)

- **Opening A** — Trial morning: guard and defense for the cohort. Seiran watches. No praise, then: "Acceptable." Jalen nearly faints with joy.
- **1 ●** Natsume signs a statement naming her omission and the questions still open. It stays available to critics even when they dislike her cooperation.
- **2 ♪** White Ash farewell night: river-tune karaoke. Yuzu, newly arrived by river, beats Haru at a balance game after he calls touring unserious. Neris is not here yet; nobody sings well.
- **Opening B** — Akari and Seiran agree to a restrained rules exchange "to settle nothing."
- **3 ★◆ Akari vs Seiran.** A clean, fast anime rivalry bout: distance, angles, a reset, a mutual stop. For one beat their timing locks and the stone rings. Both feel it. Both hate how good it felt. The result is disputed and instructive.
- **4 ●** Jalen keeps his application and asks for the amended refusal procedure. (`give`: he has passed his component. `take`: he hasn't yet, and is fiercely determined.)
- **5 ◆** Kanna's letter arrives: a dated engineering request for measurements at the river. One line underlined: *Measure the houses, not just the market.* The hall prepares to travel to Weir End. Daichi goes very quiet.
- **6 ☾** Home. Dusk, the lantern, the cohort practising in the courtyard. Daichi takes the red scarf down from the beam and washes it in a basin. A long, wordless scene.
- *Sting:* the clean scarf drying in the courtyard wind.
