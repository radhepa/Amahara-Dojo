# Chapter 7 — A Thousand Small Doors

**AUTHOR ONLY. Revision 3 final treatment, 7 October 2026. Eight weeks, 48 episodes, 16 openings.**

**Training sync:** The Lantern Form (a longer solo form in four sections) and timing (bell-cued reactions, tempo control, elbows within the form). In the fiction Daichi teaches the apprentice the Lantern Form and finally says who made it.

## Chapter contract

The alternative has to work before the story can ask anyone to risk anything for it. The pilot of a thousand small doors (local anchors that can each close) shows the real costs of shared authority: missed jobs, slow disputes, refusals that inconvenience everyone. Those failures are fixed, not blamed on enemies. Shigure's lease gives the Directorate lawful access to eastern anchors, and the Old Gate's repair slips behind freight. Then the Lantern Festival lights every door in the valley at once, the happiest night of the saga, and the rain begins.

Door chimes on the small anchors ring their own local three-note clearances. **Lantern Hall's big bell must still ring only two notes throughout this chapter.**

## Week 1 — Permission Before Connection

- **Opening A** — Weir End consent meeting, run by Kanna. A household can decline a branch without losing clinic access; the veto is tested on its first afternoon by an old fisherman who says no and keeps his medicine.
- **1 ●** Riku lists available gates and paid installation hours. The parts pool cannot connect everyone who wants it.
- **2 ♪** The cohort's door school: how a door anchor works, on an isolated model. Toma insists on carrying the heavy box; Kanna hands him the clipboard instead, which he finds even more important.
- **Opening B** — Akari wants a single checklist under her custody. Emi: "A shared system needs operators who can correct the checklist." Akari lets Emi write the second line.
- **3 ★** The first connection is approved by a household representative, an engineer and an operator. The first door is an old woman's kitchen anchor. She hangs a lantern on it: "So you know it's mine."
- **4 ●** Seiran accepts a limited academy operator team with an appeal route. Haru backs her stricter qualification standard and stays loyal to Ren in the same sentence.
- **5 ◆** Daichi begins teaching the apprentice the Lantern Form at dawn. After the first section: "Iori made this. Out of the count. He said a form is a letter you write to yourself every morning." It is the first time Daichi has said his name in the hall without being asked.
- **6 ☾** Chapter 1 `promise` flag shapes how the doors' maintenance roster is written (a dated owner per door / named shared parts with backups). Supper.
- *Sting:* the first lantern on the first door.

## Week 2 — The Job With No Name

- **Opening A** — A maintenance check is missed: each partner believed the other held it. The branch stays safely closed; a delivery waits.
- **1 ●** Shigure names the liability gap accurately. Akari cannot answer by pointing at his worse crime.
- **2 ♪** The blame round-robin, performed as comedy: every person in the hall explains why it was someone else's, until Toma produces a chart showing it was everyone's. He is unbearable for a day.
- **Opening B** — Jalen proposes a named lead, a backup and an appeal with a fixed term. His academy habits make the alternative stronger.
- **3 ★** Sora posts the failure report before the success report. The first audience criticises the pilot and still has enough to decide whether to stay in it. Most stay.
- **4 ●** The delayed delivery arrives under the corrected assignment. No sabotage twist excuses the mistake.
- **5 ◆** Lantern Form, section two. The apprentice keeps losing the turn; Daichi makes them do it facing the wall until the turn belongs to them.
- **6 ☾** Ren, at the bakery, teaches the cohort to fold dough on a count. It becomes a hall habit.
- *Sting:* Toma's chart pinned beside the year's conditions.

## Week 3 — A Bell That May Be Refused

- **Opening A** — The first live supervised test across several doors.
- **1 ●** A household calls its local stop mid-test. Central timing breaks; physical isolation prevents a surge downstream.
- **2 ♪** Tarek and Elian visit to help. Elian and Ren become friends in about four minutes over bread; Seiran and Elian resume the arm-wrestle (tie).
- **Opening B** — Tarek's and Elian's operators adapt through the established codes. Nobody pretends the anchors can carry words.
- **3 ★◆** Seiran confirms the refusal was valid though it wrecks her team's schedule. Her academy's provisional rule becomes visible behaviour under pressure. Haru looks at her as if seeing her clearly for the first time.
- **4 ●** Ren keeps the support rota through four completely uneventful sessions. Nobody cheers. "Nothing happened" becomes his proudest sentence.
- **5 ◆** The household agrees to resume. Every door chime in the pilot rings three, a small bright cascade down the street. Lantern Hall's bell, a moment later, rings two. Natsume, in the courtyard, closes her eyes.
- **6 ☾** Kanna records both the success and its extra cost in the same entry.
- *Sting:* a door chime still trembling.

## Week 4 — An Offer to Bridge the Gap

- **Opening A** — Shigure presents the eastern-anchor supply lease to the river assembly: Directorate engineers get temporary inspection and operating access in exchange for immediate repairs and freight support.
- **1 ●** Neris, now an independent observer, asks for mixed custody and a non-renewable term. The assembly inserts limits and leaves emergency renewal with the contract holder.
- **2 ♪** Mika's birthday. The founders throw a small party; Daichi bakes a cake and it collapses; Ren rescues it into something he names "The Recovery." (Daichi–Mika moment if their relationship is active; warm friendship otherwise.)
- **Opening B** — Natsume points to the bypass the renewal clause preserves, using her own omission as the evidence for why technical discretion needs visible limits.
- **3 ★** Seiran disagrees with Akari: trained central crews may be necessary in winter while local gates remain unfinished. The alliance holds through a real policy disagreement; both are partly right.
- **4 ●** Lantern Hall challenges the renewal clause publicly and Natsume negotiates independent inspectors into implementation. Both happen. Neither can stop the vote.
- **5 ◆** Shigure signs the lawful lease, believing he can keep it bounded. Written access now exists at sites the coalition cannot physically command.
- **6 ☾** Akari and Natsume's last good dinner. They laugh about the patterned cloth Natsume gave Akari when she was seven, which Akari still has and will not discuss. Write it as ordinary. Never let it read as a farewell.
- *Sting:* two cups, one with a crooked handle, left where they are.

## Week 5 — A Repair Paid by Someone

- **Opening A** — Directorate crews repair an eastern gate fast. Residents thank the workers and criticise the founders for warning without resources.
- **1 ●** Avel separates the ordinary Veyr technicians from Directorate policy and refuses a demand that she guarantee what her country will do.
- **2 ♪ Decision C7-A `lantern`.** Lantern-making day for the festival. Avel sews; Yuzu folds a paper moth for every lantern; Toma paints; Jalen's lantern is structurally unsound. Each person writes one word on their door lantern. The apprentice writes a name (someone they are doing this for), `OPEN`, or `HERE`.
- **Opening B** — Sora finds that emergency renewal could put records in the same custody as operators. Neris agrees and cannot rewrite a signed lease.
- **3 ★** The Old Gate. Its repair needs parts on the same freight lines the lease now prioritises; its rotten access beam can only be replaced once the gate is isolated with those parts. The work slips past the rains. Natsume reads the delay notice twice and says nothing at all. (A real cause; no player choice touches it.)
- **4 ●** Riku's team finishes a pilot gate with an accessible manual control. The Old Gate stays on a funded, unfinished list.
- **5 ◆** Mika secures clinic privacy in a written local arrangement. "It survives only if someone enforces it when pressure comes," she says, and signs.
- **6 ☾** Lantern Form, section three. Daichi watches without correcting once. The apprentice notices and doesn't know whether to be proud or worried.
- *Sting:* finished lanterns drying in a row, words facing the wall.

## Week 6 — The Lantern Festival

- **Opening A** — Amahara's end-of-summer festival. This year every door in the pilot, and half the doors in town, will hang a lantern.
- **1 ♪** Festival day. Akari wins the ring toss with frightening precision; Natsume is hopeless at goldfish scooping and keeps trying; Seiran and Elian compete at every stall; Jalen eats everything in his notebook; Toma wins a prize and gives it to Mika; Ren runs a bread stall that sells out by noon.
- **2 ♪** The cohort's festival: Emi in the robe Avel made; Avel made everyone's; Jalen's sleeves are slightly different lengths and he hasn't noticed. A cohort portrait.
- **Opening B** — Dusk. Lanterns ready on every door.
- **3 ★ Decision C7-B `festival`.** Who does the apprentice light their lantern with? Akari, Ren, Sora, Daichi, Yuzu, or the cohort. Each is a private scene in which that person tells the apprentice one true thing they have never said aloud. Romance variant if a relationship is active (an adult confession or a promise); friendship variant otherwise, with equal weight.
- **4 ◆ A thousand doors.** At full dark, on the count, every lantern on every door is lit at once. The door chimes ring three in a wave that runs down the valley. At the end of the wave, Lantern Hall's bell rings two — the only one. Natsume stands at the Old Gate's fence, looking up at all the light. **(Key visual of the saga.)**
- **5 ☾** Walking home, Natsume to Akari: "Put the Old Gate first on the list. In ink." Akari: "It already is."
- **6 ☾** Morning after. Paper moths in the gutters, lanterns cooling, the apprentice's lantern still lit with its word.
- *Sting:* a single lantern burning in daylight.

## Week 7 — Access Is a Form of Power

- **Opening A** — Yuzu leaves for her agreed river contract with a return route and a backup courier. The chapter will not punish her for not predicting a war.
- **1 ●** Ren refuses a second commitment he can't keep; Emi asks the founders to stop calling every beginner task "the movement"; Jalen takes an exam component and records a skill he still lacks.
- **2 ♪** A small celebration with no speech. Ordinary life is what all of this is meant to protect.
- **Opening B** — Inspectors and petitioners document Directorate stores at eastern anchors beyond the repair scope. The materials could mean repair or armed custody.
- **3 ★** Neris reports that the Directorate has invoked a border-security review. Civilian negotiators can still speak; they cannot bind military deployments.
- **4 ●** Elian separates Tideglass operators from the Directorate roster and arranges a civilian route home. Veyr contains its own opposition before any occupation begins.
- **5 ◆** Kanna refuses a proposed central reconnection at Weir End until control limits are tested. The coalition backs the refusal despite the supply cost.
- **6 ☾** Shigure refuses to cancel the lease without replacement supplies. Forecast: heavy rain, scheduled for the story's next season (never tied to the player's attendance).
- *Sting:* a weather notice pinned over the festival poster.

## Week 8 — A Successful Small System (chapter trial)

- **Opening A** — Trial morning: the apprentice performs the whole Lantern Form for Daichi. Silence. Then C4-A `chamber`: "Iori would say your third step is late." "Is it?" "No. He'd just say it."
- **1 ●** The pilot handles a planned load test and a local stop across several independently run doors. Its first weakness has a working correction.
- **2 ♪** Kanna names Lantern Hall's floor anchor a pilot door. Primary keepers: trained pairs. Backups: the apprentice and Emi, newly finished with the form. Emi prints the roster in her best handwriting and then hides in the store room.
- **Opening B** — Lantern Hall's year review begins, presented in the order of the Chapter 1 `repair` choice.
- **3 ★** A dispatch: Directorate emergency crews are arriving under the lease with armed escorts. The regional government disputes the claimed scope through formal channels.
- **4 ●** Seiran adopts the pilot's refusal procedure for academy field exercises, keeps her stricter operator rules, and takes criticism for both.
- **5 ◆** Two records side by side on Akari's table: a working local consent test, and a renewal order nobody local can stop. The first raindrops land on lantern paper in the courtyard.
- **6 ☾** Rain all night. At midnight the bell rings two, then two again: a weather warning code.
- *Sting:* rain on the lanterns.

## Optional founder conversations

- **Akari:** a checklist in someone else's handwriting; discomfort with a valid refusal; assigning a real decision; accepting a correction she wouldn't have chosen.
- **Ren:** a boring rota; wanting a memorable role; declining an extra job; a shift whose only reward is that nothing went wrong.
- **Sora:** an imperfect failure report; the urge to polish it; asking witnesses to approve wording; publishing their criticism beside her summary.
- **Daichi:** a delayed coordinator reply; wanting to settle an argument alone; agreeing to wait; watching the procedure solve it differently.
- **Yuzu:** a packed case; guilt at unfinished work; naming a return and a substitute; leaving without a loyalty test.

## Handoff

The distributed design works at small scale with honest costs. Lantern Hall's door is part of it, with the apprentice and Emi as backups. The Directorate has lawful physical access to eastern anchors. The Old Gate's repair and its beam wait behind freight. No dialogue choice removes the danger or makes any later loss the player's fault.
