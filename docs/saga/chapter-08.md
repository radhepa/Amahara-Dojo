# Chapter 8 — The Long Rain

**AUTHOR ONLY. Revision 3 final treatment, 7 October 2026. Six weeks, 36 episodes, 12 openings. Contains a mandatory death, permanent injury and occupation.**

**Training sync:** Endurance and composure (longer rounds within the time cap, breathing under fatigue, steady form when tired). In the fiction the apprentice and Emi hold the hall's anchor for ninety seconds.

## Chapter contract

A real storm arrives while the Directorate holds the eastern anchors. Maela invokes emergency control, cancels local suspension and occupies the watershed's strategic sites. Seven chapters of preparation save most of what can be saved and cannot save everything. The apprentice faces their first real test. White Ash falls with every student alive. Mika's hand and Ren's knee are hurt. At the Old Gate, Natsume frees the jammed mechanism by hand; Lantern Hall's bell rings three notes for the first time the player has ever heard; the last evacuees cross on it; and she dies. A full final week follows the survivors so grief is lived, not used as a cliffhanger.

Write fewer ♪ episodes here. Replace them with small warmth: a joke that fails, a shared bowl, someone sleeping on a shoulder.

None of these losses depend on any player choice, attendance or relationship. Say so through structure: no option in this chapter touches the Old Gate, the clinic crossing or the academy.

## Week 1 — The Renewal That Arrives Armed

- **Opening A** — Rain raises known loads along the eastern route. Responders issue ordinary warnings and closures using everything prepared. The hall suspends practice for the first time.
- **1 ●** Maela invokes the lease's renewal power and announces compulsory unified custody. The regional assembly objects. Directorate units are already holding the anchor sites before anyone can adjudicate.
- **2 ☾** Neris rejects the cancellation of local vetoes publicly and is stripped of every remaining negotiating role. She walks out into the rain without an umbrella and Sora goes after her with two.
- **Opening B** — Elian's letter: Tideglass operators have refused incorporation into the occupation roster.
- **3 ★** Shigure reads the order twice. "That isn't what I signed." A dispatch from Weir End arrives in the same hour, two lines in Kanna's hand: *It is exactly what you signed. I am closing our doors.*
- **4 ●** The hall becomes a temporary coordination room and shelter. Chapter 1 `access` flag shapes it (open hall with a quiet corner / bookable spaces with flexible requests). Those who won't help still get shelter.
- **5 ◆** C6-A `role`: the apprentice takes the shelter door, greeting and placing arrivals (`people`), or the notice-and-bell desk with Emi (`signal`). Chapter 1 `mistake` flag shapes how notices are corrected. Jalen runs the coordinator backup he designed in Chapter 7.
- **6 ☾** Night in the hall: everyone asleep on mats except the apprentice and Ren. Ren says something honest without a joke attached.
- *Sting:* thirty pairs of wet shoes at the door, no labels.

## Week 2 — The Doors Already Built

- **Opening A** — Kanna isolates the Weir End pilot before the central surge reaches it. The refusal hardware critics called wasteful now protects every household on it.
- **1 ●** The coordinator structure handles assignments with its known strengths and delays. Jalen fixes a gap using the agreed backup and nobody notices, which is the point.
- **2 ☾** Yuzu, away on contract, receives the dispatch through her established courier. Her return will take days. She starts walking anyway.
- **Opening B** — Every trained person in the hall is out at the sites. Riku comes back alone, soaked: the hall's floor anchor is coupled and shuddering under the surge.
- **3 ★◆ The first test.** Riku needs ninety seconds to set a brace and nobody trained is left. The listed backups are the apprentice and Emi. They stand where the yellow cord used to be and hold the base count on the floor they helped repair. The stone hums under their feet. Riku counts down from the brace, not from the clock. At forty the apprentice's knees shake; at twenty Emi starts crying and doesn't stop counting. At zero the brace bites. They sit down on the floor and laugh and cry together. Riku sits with them. "That's ninety. You held ninety."
- **4 ●** Residents and the cohort carry the hall's contents to the school shelter. Sora's copy custody plan activates: duplicates leave with the people, originals with independent keepers. The hall is closed for inspection.
- **5 ◆ Decision C8-A `toma`.** In the shelter, Toma wants to go out and help. He is fourteen. The apprentice gives him a real job inside the shelter (`job`) or sits with him and admits they're scared too (`stay`). Both keep him safe.
- **6 ☾** The regional government mobilises lawful responders outside the occupied sites. Access is constrained, not absent.
- *Sting:* two pairs of footprints in the dust of the empty hall floor.

## Week 3 — White Ash Empties

- **Opening A** — Directorate officers demand the academy's route records and operator list. Seiran refuses enrolment of her trainees and starts the practised evacuation before arguing custody.
- **1 ●** Haru leads qualified operators while students leave by the known protected route. They have drilled this. It shows.
- **2 ☾** A Directorate officer demands the trainee roster from Jalen at the records table. Jalen, voice cracking: "Under section four, this roster is protected." He doesn't move. (C3-A `give`: afterwards, "Someone gave me their turn once. I figured I owed one.")
- **Opening B** — Maela's crew holds the academy anchor under storm load, insisting release would disrupt supply. Seiran documents the order and the refusal.
- **3 ★◆** Akari's team covers a decoupled perimeter so the last empty rooms can be swept. Seiran is the last one out of the building.
- **4 ●** The empty academy fails under the unreleased load. Records kept only there are lost; the training floor is lost; the building is gone.
- **5 ◆** Roll call beside a temporary frame in the rain. Every name answers. Seiran says class will resume somewhere else. She cannot turn round to look at the building. Akari, beside her: "I'll look for you." And does, and tells her it's gone, and Seiran nods once.
- **6 ☾** Students asleep in a borrowed barn, Seiran awake at the door like a lantern.
- *Sting:* a white sash on a fence post, left to dry.

## Week 4 — The Clinic Crossing

- **Opening A** — Mika's clinic evacuates across a narrowed landing while conventional crews brace the span. Patient privacy survives in sealed records and minimal public lists.
- **1 ●** Ren and Haru rotate a trained containment task; Daichi supports the brace crew; the apprentice works the shelter (by `role`) as patients arrive.
- **2 ☾** A stretcher jams on the landing.
- **Opening B** — The crossing narrows further.
- **3 ★◆** Mika frees the path by hand and shifting hardware crushes her left hand. Her colleagues take over without a word. Seconds later Ren stops a slipping brace within his task and his knee goes. Haru calls his relief before Ren can try again (C4-B `corner`: "Not today, laughing boy." / "Stay with the plan, Ren.").
- **4 ●** Shelter placement follows clinical triage and guaranteed safe space; households and services are grouped by Mika's colleagues, who know the patients.
- **5 ◆** The patients reach shelter. Daichi sees Mika's hand and is not permitted to make her treatment a test of how much she needs him. She asks him to fetch her spectacles. He does.
- **6 ☾** Ren on a cot, leg raised, eyes on the door. The apprentice brings water. He makes a joke. It fails. "I'm scared." The apprentice stays.
- *Sting:* Ren's wraps on the floor beside the cot.

## Week 5 — The Third Note

- **Opening A** — The Old Gate cannot be decoupled safely while Maela's eastern anchor stays loaded. Remote release would push the surge toward occupied houses below the market. Natsume names the manual fallback; she is the qualified inspector on site.
- **1 ●** Regional responders request that the Directorate release the eastern anchor for twenty minutes. Maela refuses: the freight corridor first. The last evacuation group, the overflow from the lower streets, waits at the crossing below the Old Gate.
- **2 ☾** Natsume clips on her tether. Riku marks the access beam and a withdrawal limit; both of them know what the beam is. She tells the crew: "When you hear three, send them across." Akari: "Check it twice." Natsume: "I always do." She hands Akari her inspection pencil to hold, as if she'll want it back.
- **Opening B** — In the shelter the apprentice is at their post. The bell has rung two notes all their life in Amahara.
- **3 ★◆ The third note.** Trained pairs hold short stabilisation intervals at the crossing. Natsume works the lever in the shaft. The gate jams. She frees it. The Old Gate clears, and Lantern Hall's bell rings three notes. Then every bell in the valley. In the shelter people stand up without knowing why. At the crossing the last evacuees go on the third note, all of them.
- **4 ●** As the last one reaches the far side, the access beam fails. The tethered extraction crew is close and not close enough; they cannot release the loaded gate without striking the people who just crossed. Their attempt is visible and finite. Through the noise, Natsume's voice: "It works."
- **5 ◆** The route holds. Natsume is dead. Say it plainly. No missing body, no ambiguity, no later reversal. Akari on the bank with the pencil.
- **6 ☾** The shelter heard three notes and cheered. Then the news comes in. The apprentice remembers their first day, Ren and Akari at the window: *They used to ring three.* The bell keeps ringing three through the night at intervals, because the route is finally clear.
- *Sting:* the inspection pencil in Akari's hand.

## Week 6 — After the Count (chapter trial)

- **Opening A** — Trial morning: composure. The hall is closed; the cohort practises the base form slowly in the shelter yard. Nobody speaks. Nobody needs to.
- **1 ●** Evacuees are counted; crews mark what remains unsafe. Akari reorganises everyone's tasks and does not eat.
- **2 ☾** Emi confronts her: the room needs a leader who can accept a shift, not another person running it through exhaustion.
- **Opening B** — Akari eats, sitting on the floor. Emi sits beside her.
- **3 ★ Decision C8-B `memorial`.** Natsume's memorial. Akari asks the apprentice whether the bell should ring three at the end (`bell`) or stay silent for a full day (`silence`). C6-B `names`: the apprentice reads Natsume's name (`read`) or Akari reads it while the apprentice stands back as at Merrow (`alone`). C3-B `seat`: Akari stands where she sat at White Ash, with the family or among the town. Residents may criticise the signature and mourn the woman; neither is corrected. Iori's condolence is read: "She signed it. She held the gate. Both."
- **4 ●** Ren begins rehabilitation and hands the support rota to others. Mika learns the lasting limitation in her hand and starts planning adapted work with her colleagues. Neither Daichi nor the apprentice prescribes anything.
- **5 ◆** The storm eases; the checkpoints stay. Temporary custody has become occupation. Elian and Veyr civilian organisers protect supplies and refuse conscription lists. Neris brings a signed order overriding local vetoes and names where the Crown Junction operating logs are kept. Yuzu walks in along a permitted route, muddy and furious at herself, and is allowed to be angry. Shigure admits the Directorate went beyond what he expected; Kanna, arrived from Weir End once its doors were safe: admission is not acquittal.
- **6 ☾** A sheltered meal, awkward and warm in parts. Neris tells them one more thing: when Maela was told who held the Old Gate, she walked out of her own briefing and did not come back for an hour.
- *Sting:* `bell` — three notes at dawn; `silence` — a still bell rope, and the next dawn Akari rings it herself.

## Handoff

The losses are confirmed and permanent. The academy's people survived its building; Natsume did not. Mika and Ren need changed work, not miracles. The occupation has a finite geography; wider institutions contest it; the coalition has evidence, allies and a location for the logs. The apprentice has held ninety seconds. The bell rings three now.
